I’m Noah Abdallah — a multidisciplinary art director and senior designer working across identity, packaging, campaign systems, motion and production.

I’ve spent more than a decade moving between screen and physical output. That means I care about the idea, the system behind it, and what actually happens when the work leaves the artboard.

My approach is simple: define the visual logic, make it repeatable, then push the execution until the system feels intentional at every scale.

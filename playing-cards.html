Cedars
Playing Cards.

A complete custom deck inspired by Lebanon's cedar tree and cultural heritage — designed as one coherent system across courts, pips, jokers, the Ace of Spades, card backs and tuck packaging.

2021Kickstarter
Launch
ProjectCedars Playing Cards
RoleDeck Designer
Scope56-card custom deck + tuck
ProductionWJPC

The idea

Turn a national symbol into a functional deck — recognizable in play, culturally specific in detail, and consistent enough to feel like a real product rather than a collection of individual illustrations.

The system

The deck combines customized ARRCO court cards and pips with a fully customized Ace of Spades, a two-way back design, custom jokers and a matte tuck case.
